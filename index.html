Bloom

Small intentions, quiet growth.

Getting started

Each morning, type the few things that matter today and tap Add. Tap an intention to mark it done. Long-press one to remove it.

Widgets

Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and choose Bloom. The medium widget lets you tick intentions off without opening the app.

Live Activity

In Bloom, open Settings and turn on Show on Lock Screen. If it doesn't appear, check Settings › Bloom › Live Activities on your iPhone.

Siri

Try "How's my Bloom?", "Add an intention in Bloom" or "Tick off morning walk in Bloom".

Changing what you grow

Open Settings in Bloom and tap What you grow to pick from 15 cells, flowers and plants. Your progress carries over.
