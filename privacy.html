Privacy Policy

Bloom: Daily Intentions · Effective 20 November 2026

The short version

Bloom does not collect any data. Everything you type stays on your iPhone.

What Bloom stores

Your intentions, your chosen design and your settings are saved on your device only, so the app, its widgets and its Live Activity can show them. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

What Bloom does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising
  • No third-party code that collects data
  • No sharing or selling of information

Siri

If you use Bloom with Siri, Apple processes your request under Apple's own privacy policy. Bloom only receives the intention you asked to add or complete, and keeps it on your device.

Deleting your data

Deleting Bloom from your iPhone removes everything it stored.

Children

Bloom collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
